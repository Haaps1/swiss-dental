Our Story

A Clinic Built On Trust, Comfort & Precision

Swiss Dental opened its doors off Sarjapura Road in 2011 with a simple promise — dentistry that feels calm, honest and genuinely caring. What began as a small practice by Dr. Vinay Vindas has grown into a full multi‑disciplinary clinic covering nine dental specialties under one roof.

Today, our team blends modern technology — digital X‑Ray, laser dentistry, Invisalign — with an unhurried, patient‑first philosophy that has earned the trust of over 10,000 patients across Bangalore.
